  1. 01

    Pick a cow

    Every pump.fun coin that graduates gets a PumpSwap pool. MILK checks each one on-chain and ranks the real ones by what they paid liquidity providers in the last day.

  2. 02

    Add from SOL

    MILK swaps about half your SOL into the coin, then adds both sides to the pool in the same transaction. If the price moves past your limit, nothing happens and you keep your SOL.

  3. 03

    Every trade pays the pool

    Each trade leaves an LP fee in the pool, and your share grows with it. The LP tokens sit in your wallet, so you can withdraw here or on pump.fun.

05 · FAQ

The obvious questions

Can I lose money?

Yes. A position holds half SOL and half coin. If the coin falls, the pool sells you more of it on the way down, so you can end up with less SOL than you put in, even after fees. This is called impermanent loss, and it is the main risk. The pool panel shows what your position would be worth after a price move before you add.

Where does the milk come from?

From trading fees. Every PumpSwap trade pays a fee, and part of it, the LP fee, stays in the pool for liquidity providers. On pump.fun pools the LP fee is 0.20% of each trade once the coin's market cap is above about 420 SOL, and 0.02% below that. MILK reads each pool's current rate from PumpSwap's on-chain fee config.

Does MILK hold my funds?

No. MILK builds a standard PumpSwap transaction, checks it in a simulation, and your own wallet signs it. LP tokens go straight to your wallet. MILK has no contract of its own and cannot move your funds.

What does MILK charge?

Adding and withdrawing are free on MILK. You only pay Solana network fees and a small, refundable rent for new token accounts.

Which pools can I milk?

Canonical PumpSwap pools of pump.fun coins paired with SOL. MILK only lists pools it finds and checks on-chain, so copycat pools never make the board. Paste any coin address to find its pool. Coins still on the bonding curve have no pool yet.

Why did my add fail?

Usually the price moved more than your limit between the quote and the transaction landing. Nothing is taken except the network fee. Try again, or allow a bigger price move on fast coins.

Is milk / day a promise?

No. It is what 1 SOL added at today's price would have earned in LP fees over the last 24 hours. Volume changes every minute, your share shrinks if others add after you, and price moves are not included.

Is there a $MILK token?

Not yet. Any contract address posted before it appears on this page is not ours.
