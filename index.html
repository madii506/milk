03 · How it works

Three steps, one signature

  1. Pick a cow

    Every pump.fun coin that graduates gets a PumpSwap pool. MILK checks each pool on-chain and ranks the real ones by how much they paid liquidity providers in the last day.

  2. Add from SOL

    MILK swaps about half your SOL into the coin, then adds both sides to the pool, all in one transaction. If the price moves past your slippage, nothing happens and you keep your SOL.

  3. Every trade pays the pool

    Each trade leaves an LP fee in the pool, and your share of the pool grows with it. Your LP tokens sit in your wallet, so you can withdraw here or on pump.fun.

04 · FAQ

The obvious questions

Can I lose money?

Yes. When you add liquidity you hold half SOL and half coin. If the coin drops, the pool sells you more of it on the way down, and you can end up with less than you put in even after fees. This is called impermanent loss, and it is the main risk. Memecoins move fast, so only add what you are fine holding as the coin.

Where does the milk come from?

From trading fees. PumpSwap charges every trade a fee, and part of it, the LP fee, stays in the pool for liquidity providers. On pump.fun pools the LP fee is 0.20% of each trade once the coin's market cap is above about 420 SOL, and 0.02% below that. MILK reads each pool's current rate from PumpSwap's on-chain fee config.

Does MILK hold my funds?

No. MILK builds a standard PumpSwap transaction and your own wallet signs it. The LP tokens go straight to your wallet. MILK has no contract of its own and cannot move your funds. You can withdraw on MILK or on pump.fun.

What does MILK charge?

0.1% of each add goes to the MILK jug. Withdrawals are free. You also pay Solana network fees and a small, refundable rent for new token accounts.

Which pools can I milk?

Canonical PumpSwap pools of pump.fun coins paired with SOL. MILK only lists pools it can find and verify on-chain, so copycat pools and fake volume never make the list. Paste any coin address to find its pool. Coins still on the bonding curve have no pool yet.

Why did my add fail?

Usually the price moved more than your slippage between the quote and the transaction landing. Nothing is taken except the network fee. Try again, or raise slippage a little on fast-moving coins.

Is the milk / day number a promise?

No. It is the last 24 hours of fees divided by the pool's size. Volume changes every minute, and your share shrinks if others add after you.
